Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. You can plan a party without signing in. Sign in (free) to keep your plan on all your devices, send invitations and track RSVPs.

How do guests reply to an invitation?
They open the link you send. No app or account needed. Their replies show up in the Guests tab.

I'm a photographer, decorator or other helper. How do I get listed?
In the app, open Account → Offer a service and create your free listing.

How do I cancel an event?
On Home, tap ⋯ → Cancel this event. You can tell your guests with a short note, and bring the event back later from My events.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, open Account → Delete account. This removes your events, listing and login for good.
