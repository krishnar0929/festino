Festino

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Festino ("we") is a party and event planning app by Northbit Apps. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Account details if you sign in: your email address and name, from email sign-in, Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Your events and plans: occasion, date, time, venue, guest count, checklist, budget, games and notes you add.
  • Invitations: the card text, photos and videos you add, and the guests you invite (names, phone numbers or emails you enter or pick from your contacts). We only save the guests you choose; your contact list itself is never uploaded.
  • RSVPs: when guests reply to your invitation, we save their name, reply and any note they leave.
  • Location: only while you use the app, to set your party venue, show the weather for party day and find helpers nearby.
  • Helper listings, quotes and reviews: if you list a business, request a quote or write a review, we save what you enter.
  • Usage information: searches and filters you use in the app, with your device type, app version and IP address, so we can improve results.
  • Feedback you send us.

How we use it

  • To build and save your party plan, send your invitations and show you who's coming.
  • To show the weather and helpers near your venue.
  • To send the reminders you turn on.
  • To answer your questions and make Festino better.

Who else handles it

  • Google Firebase stores your account, plans and uploads, and hosts our website.
  • Open-Meteo receives your venue's location to return the weather forecast.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose those options.
  • Guests you invite see the invitation details you choose to share. Helpers you contact see the details you send them.

Your choices

  • You can use Festino without an account.
  • You can turn off location, photos, camera, contacts and notifications in your phone's Settings at any time.
  • You can edit or delete any event, and cancel or delete invitations.
  • Delete your account: in the app, Account → Delete account. This removes your events, listing and login. You can also email us to ask.

Children

Festino is meant for adults planning events. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for big changes, let you know in the app.
